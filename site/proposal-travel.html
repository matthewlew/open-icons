What is a campground restroom?

decide

The app carries plumbing status in its amenity data, but not every site has that field filled in — and the icon has to render either way. A is the default: it never asserts a plumbing claim the data can't back up. B and C are drawn from that same data, only when it actually says flush or vault — they are specific overlays, not competing defaults.

A · figures — recommended default

Male now takes _person’s shoulder curve verbatim; female’s skirt is bowed, not a hard triangle, so both bodies carry the same optical mass. Says “restroom here”, not “plumbing” — which is exactly the claim to make when the data doesn't say either way.

B · flush — shown when the data confirms it

Says plumbing, running water, a sink. Reads at 16px because the tank/bowl mass is two blocks, not seven strokes.

C · vault / porta-potty — shown when the data confirms it

Says no plumbing. The vent stack is the tell. Needed alongside B, not instead of it.

Is there a universal EV symbol?

decide

No ISO mark the way there is for recycling — but the National Park Service ships one, in the public-domain nationalparkservice/symbol-library repo. Their electric-car-charging-black-22.svg is a pump cabinet with the bolt as its readout, and their electrical-hookup-black-30.svg — the campground power-pedestal symbol — is a pair of straight prongs on a stem, not a bent gas nozzle. A below now shares fuel's actual tank silhouette (same body, same ground line) and ends in that prong pair instead of a hose hook. All three reuse ONE bolt path, scaled — not three different bolts — and the containers are pulled in from the standard 5.14 container radius so neither reads as a rounded badge.

A · pump + bolt + plug — recommended

Body is fuel's tank, verbatim — same primitive, so "is this a pump" is true by construction, not by eye. Bolt is the standalone bolt path at half scale, centered on the tank's own center (9.1, 12.55) by calculation. Tail ends in two straight prongs — ev-plug's own line grammar, not a nozzle.

B · connector head

Flat bottom, rounded shoulders, three prongs — reads as a plug housing now rather than a stadium-shaped badge. Same bolt path again, at about a third scale.

C · bolt in a container

The original. First draft used bolt completely unscaled, which actually overshot this 17×17 box — its tips punched through the rounded corners top and bottom. Same center (12,12), scaled to 0.72 so it fits with real clearance. Cheapest, least specific of the three.

Parking: circle or square?

decide

The NPS parking pictogram settles this by omission: it is a bare P, no container shape at all — they rely on a colored placard tile instead of drawing a ring or box into the glyph. Real road signage (the blue P placard) is square, never circular, on every system this project would sit next to. Recommend square.

A · square — recommended

Matches road signage and the set's own container idiom (add-square, check-square). Radius pulled to 3, matching the EV containers.

B · circle

Closer to some map products' style, but matches neither real signage nor this set's existing containers. Kept here for comparison only.

Line / fill pairs

the layer-toggle state

The fill never exceeds the line variant’s outer edge — 0 of the 378 existing pairs break that, and the pack holds it. Figures (campground, mountain, water) take outer−0.5 with the contact datum pinned; containers (ev-charger) take a solid at the same radius with the line glyph reused verbatim as a knockout.

Open questions

blocking
  1. Scope, renamed to facilities. The classification is binary — Generic or Product. campground is neither: not universal, but not Roadtrip-specific either. Was proposing a travel scope; most of what's actually in it — restroom, water, shower, parking, EV charging, the campground family — reads as site facilities, not travel in general, so facilities names the pack for what it actually contains. car/rv/trailer are the odd ones out under that name and may want to move to a separate vehicles group. Blocks the POI and amenity groups.
  2. Resolved: store and cart both ship. A place vs. an action — not a duplicate.
  3. Three drawings became decisions — restroom, EV, parking — and are drawn above rather than guessed at. Restroom is resolved (figures, default); EV and parking still have a recommendation each but aren't settled.
  4. park vs mountain at 16px. Two gable-derived silhouettes may not separate. See the 16px strip above before committing.
  5. Figure or container fill per POI icon. Confirm before generating.
