Icons that adapt,
not icons you override.

Pick a weight and every glyph redraws itself from scratch — gaps, corners, counters, all of it — the way a real type family holds its shape from thin to bold. Most icon sets give you one drawing and a stroke-width to fight with. This gives you an axis.

Why

Most icon systems are Figma-first: every glyph is a designer's hand-tuned decision, which buys craft but costs speed — a new icon means a design review, not a request. Open Icons puts the clock in the system instead of the person. Every glyph is a function of its stroke rather than a fixed drawing, so weight, size and new icons are fast to generate and fast to check, without losing the nuance a hand-drawn set gets right.
